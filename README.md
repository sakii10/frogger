# 3D Frogger (First-Person)

A first-person take on the classic Frogger arcade game, built with Unity. Cross busy roads, hop onto floating platforms to get over a river, and collect all the coins without getting hit by a car or falling into the water.

## Game Overview

The level is a hand-sculpted valley surrounded by hills. Cars race along two roads, and a river in the middle can only be crossed by riding moving wooden platforms. Scattered around the map are coins. Touching one marks it as collected and sends you back to the start, so every coin is a new run. Collect them all to win and restart the game.

## Features

- **Sculpted terrain** – Hills, riverbed, painted ground textures, trees, bushes and grass built with Unity's Terrain tools
- **First-person controls** – Walk, run and jump with the first-person controller
- **Spawning cars** – Cars appear at random intervals on two roads, driving in opposite directions
- **Floating platforms** – Moving platforms on the river carry the player across
- **Caves** – Cars disappear into cave entrances at the end of each road
- **Deadly river and traffic** – Falling into the water or getting hit restarts the level
- **Coin collection** – Coins change color when collected and send you back to the start
- **Win condition** – Collect every coin to finish, then the game restarts after a short delay
- **Invisible barriers** – Keep the player inside the playable area
